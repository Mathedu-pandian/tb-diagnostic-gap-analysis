💡 Key Model Takeaways

1. Financing & Diagnostic Infrastructure Rule Detection

Laboratory budget per 100k population and rapid diagnostic test (RDx) coverage are the strongest linear and non-linear predictors of case detection rate.

2. Funding Dependency Bottleneck

High donor dependency (>75%) correlates strongly with erratic diagnostic coverage, emphasizing the market fit for self-sustaining, low-unit-cost point-of-care kits.

3. Preserved Out-of-Fold Performance

Country-held-out validation ensures the Random Forest model generalizes to un-surveyed regions without overfitting to specific national health systems.

🧩 K-Means Country Segmentation (K=4 Archetypes)

Cluster 1: High Budget, High Diagnostic Gap (Prime LFA Target)

9 Countries (e.g. Philippines, PNG, Angola) with high mean incidence (472/100k) and ~50% diagnostic gaps despite top lab budgets. GTM Strategy: High-throughput POC triage

Cluster 2: Severely Under-Resourced & Weak Infrastructure

Countries with minimal lab funding per capita and low treatment success. GTM Strategy: Global Fund donor-backed procurement

Cluster 3: Low Rapid Test Access, Moderate Performance

Higher baseline lab capacity but missing rapid POC tests. GTM Strategy: Direct replacement of direct microscopy

Cluster 4: High Performing / Low Burden Systems

Low incidence, high case detection rate (>85%). GTM Strategy: Surveillance & low-volume screening

📋 Product Strategy Case Study: LFA Diagnostic Go-To-Market

Connecting bench-top antibody engineering with global market intelligence.

1. Executive Vision

To eliminate the 3+ million annual undetected tuberculosis cases by deploying low-cost, gold-nanoparticle polyclonal antibody lateral flow immunoassays (Ag85B antigen) into health systems with the highest diagnostic infrastructure friction.

2. Product Value Proposition & Specifications

  • Target Antigen: Ag85B Secreted Biomarker
  • Analytical Sensitivity (LOD): 0.6 ng/mL ($R^2 = 0.972$)
  • Form Factor: Equipment-free, cassette-based lateral flow test (readout in <15 minutes)
  • Cost Target: <$1.50 per strip (vs $10–30 for PCR/GeneXpert)

3. Key Strategic PM Metrics (OKRs & KPIs)

  • Target Market Reach: Top 20 priority countries covering >65% of global undetected cases.
  • Diagnostic Yield Impact: Increase baseline case detection rate in target countries from ~45% to >75%.
  • Procurement Efficiency: Reduce diagnostic cost per detected case by 60% in high donor-dependent regions.
