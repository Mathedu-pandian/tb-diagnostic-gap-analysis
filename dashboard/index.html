💡 Key Model Takeaways

1. Financing & Lab Budget Threshold Effect

Laboratory budget per 100k population and rapid diagnostic test (RDx) coverage are the strongest predictors of case detection rate.

2. Donor Dependency Bottleneck

High donor dependency (>75%) correlates strongly with erratic diagnostic coverage, emphasizing the market fit for self-sustaining, low-unit-cost point-of-care kits.

3. Preserved Out-of-Fold Performance

Country-held-out validation ensures the Random Forest model generalizes to un-surveyed regions without overfitting to specific national health systems.

🎯 Mission Objective & Executive Summary

Every year, over 3.0 million active Tuberculosis (TB) cases estimated by the WHO remain undetected globally. This report bridges benchtop bio-engineering (gold nanoparticle polyclonal antibody LFA for the Ag85B antigen; LOD = 0.6 ng/mL, R² = 0.972 at IIT Madras) with global epidemiological data science to establish an evidence-based deployment strategy targeting high-friction health systems.

1. Translational Value Chain

PHASE 1: R&D & ASSAY

Ag85B LFA Assay

Polyclonal antibody gold nanoparticle cassette with LOD of 0.6 ng/mL (R² = 0.972).

PHASE 2: MARKET DATA

Random Forest & SHAP

24-year WHO panel ingestion (2000–2024), feature engineering, and out-of-fold ML modeling (R² = 0.50).

PHASE 3: TARGET DEPLOYMENT

Archetypes & Scoring

K-Means clustering (K=4), composite priority index, and Phase 1 target market rollout.

2. Economic Market Fit & Diagnostic Bottlenecks

⚠️ Centralized Lab Bottlenecks

Conventional diagnostics rely on sputum microscopy (low sensitivity ~50%) or molecular GeneXpert PCR ($10–$30/test, continuous power requirement, specialized operators). In LMICs, patient loss-to-follow-up occurs before lab results return.

💡 Point-of-Care (POC) Value Proposition

Our cassette LFA operates equipment-free, delivers visual diagnostic readouts in <15 minutes, and targets a unit cost of <$1.50 per strip, making community screening viable for donor-dependent health systems.

5. Composite Priority Index Ranking Formula

The LFA Priority Index combines four normalized health system indicators into a transparent screening metric:

Priority Score = 0.40(Gap %) + 0.30(Incidence/100k) + 0.20(1 - RDx Access) + 0.10(1 - Lab Budget Share)

6. Institutional Strategic Decisions (IIT Madras)

  • Explainable ML Choice: Random Forest + SHAP interpretability selected over deep learning to provide clear, actionable feature rules to health ministry procurement officials.
  • 2021 Baseline Cross-Section: Selected due to optimal data completeness across all four WHO datasets (burden, notifications, outcomes, and budgets).
  • Economic Target Alignment: Aligned analytical sensitivity (LOD 0.6 ng/mL) with a strict unit cost ceiling of <$1.50 per test to thrive in donor-dependent health systems.
  • Phased Market Rollout: Prioritized Cluster 1 high-burden/high-gap nations (Papua New Guinea, Myanmar, Philippines) for Phase 1 clinical trial sites.
